Fuel Flowmeter Flanges

Michigan Aeronautical Science Association (MASA) · Limelight rocket
2025 – Present

Designed and sized a dual-sealed (O-ring + ORB) fuel flowmeter flange for the Limelight rocket in Siemens NX. Wall thickness and material were sized using hoop-stress calculations against yield and ultimate strength, and O-ring / ORB dimensions were selected from SAE face-seal gland standards to match the sized geometry.

Independently ran a bolted-joint margin-of-safety analysis on the six-bolt flange joint to verify the design before it went to manufacture:

1,050 psi static pressure 6-bolt joint 10.9% min. margin 222% joint separation margin

The finished part was machined from Aluminum 6061-T6 and pressure- and flow-tested.

Igniter Assembly & Integration — Limelight Launch Campaign

Michigan Aeronautical Science Association (MASA)
May 2026

Selected as a freshman for Limelight's launch trip team, a two-week trip to the Mojave Desert that was the culmination of the coldflow campaign and everything leading up to it. On site, I was responsible for handling and assembling APCP (ammonium perchlorate composite propellant) igniters, wiring the short-circuit ignition system, and mechanically integrating igniters into the rocket engine using nylon rod-and-nut hardware ahead of launch.

The trip tested the team well before the rocket ever left the ground. On the drive out, multiple cars broke down; when one gave out in the middle of the New Mexico desert, we made the best of the delay by exploring a giant dinosaur statue park nearby, and I got trained and certified to drive a truck with a trailer since the team was short on people who could. When the launch itself didn't work, I was genuinely devastated and leaned hard on my team — but once I'd worked through it and recognized it as an unfortunate circumstance rather than anything I'd caused, I turned back to that same instinct from the road: we got a cake, decorated it with "Limelight," and had the whole team over to the house I was staying at in Ridgecrest for a night that turned out to be a good one despite everyone being heartbroken just hours before.
